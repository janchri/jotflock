Share a board with an invitation

Choose Copy invitation and send the link to a teammate. They paste it into Join a board in Jotflock. An editor listed in the invitation must be online and sharing the board for a new teammate to join.

Each editor has a copy on their device and can keep editing offline. Changes merge when editors reconnect, and live sessions are encrypted.
